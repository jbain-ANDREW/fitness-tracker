# Fitness Strategy

## Mission

Build a sustainable gym habit while protecting a recovering back.

Success is measured by consistency rather than intensity.

## Current Routine

- Wednesday: 30-minute trainer session
  - 15 min recumbent bike
  - TRX/body-weight:
    - Inclined rows
    - Inclined bicep curls
    - Inclined chest press
    - Inclined tricep press
    - Supported squats
  - Two sets of fifteen repetitions

- Tuesday and Sunday:
  - LA Fitness with Lisa

## Design Principles

- Never leave because equipment is occupied.
- Every exercise should have alternatives.
- Prefer dumbbells and open space.
- Protect the back.
- Reduce decision fatigue.
- Simplicity beats optimization.

## Workout Architecture

Each workout should include:

- Push
- Pull
- Legs
- Core
- Cardio

Exercise selection is flexible.

## Exercise Hierarchy

Level 1
- Dumbbells
- One bench

Level 2
- Body weight

Level 3
- Empty floor space

Every workout should degrade gracefully.
